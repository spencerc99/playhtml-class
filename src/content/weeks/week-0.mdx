## Setup your computer

Download and setup the following softwares:

- [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) or another code editor of your choice. This is where you will write your code and build your site. Some other options: [Zed](https://zed.dev/), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/), Sublime Text, etc.
- [Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/) or your preferred browse of choice. Chromium-based browsers are ideal.
- [GitHub Desktop](https://desktop.github.com/) or another Git client of your choice. This is how you will use to push your code to GitHub to keep track of your work.
- Create an account on [GitHub](https://github.com/) if you don't have one already. This is where you will host your code and share it with us and your classmates.

## What's the "bench" you want to create on the web?

Please read the following two short articles before class. We'll discuss them at the start!

- "Why the disappearance of digital third spaces matters" by Nuzhat Tahiya](https://www.thedailystar.net/star-youth/news/why-the-disappearance-digital-third-spaces-matters-4084946): on the transformation of the web
- ["On Contamination" by Kim Kleinert](https://www.are.na/editorial/on-contamination): on digital zones of encounter.

While you're reading, we encourage you to think about what physical communal spaces are inspiring to you and what kind of digital encounter you want to facilitate. Think about the times you have felt intimate with people online and the kinds of interpersonal relationships you are designing for (strangers, friends, regulars to the same space). What attributes, interactions, or elements define that space?
